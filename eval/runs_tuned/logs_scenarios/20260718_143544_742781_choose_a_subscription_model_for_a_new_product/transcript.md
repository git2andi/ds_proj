# Choose a subscription model for a new productivity app

A team of five is selecting a subscription model for a new productivity app. The options vary in billing frequency, price per user, and included features to accommodate different user needs and budgets.

## Options

- A) Monthly Subscription Plan — billing frequency: monthly; price per user: 5 USD; included features: basic productivity tools; cancellation policy: cancel anytime (+ flexible commitment with monthly payments; − higher total cost over long-term use)
- B) Annual Subscription Plan — billing frequency: annual; price per user: 50 USD; included features: basic productivity tools plus priority support; cancellation policy: no refund after 30 days (+ lower price per month with added support; − less flexibility due to yearly commitment)
- C) Freemium Model with Paid Upgrades — billing frequency: none for free tier; price per user: 0 USD for free, upgrades available; included features: limited tools in free tier; upgrade options: monthly or annual (+ no initial cost with option to upgrade; − limited features may require paid upgrade)
- D) Pay-As-You-Go Model — billing frequency: per usage; price per user: 0.10 USD per task completed; included features: full tool access; minimum commitment: none (+ pay only for what you use; − cost can be unpredictable with high usage)
