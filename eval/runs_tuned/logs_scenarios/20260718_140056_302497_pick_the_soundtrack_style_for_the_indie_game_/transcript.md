# Pick the soundtrack style for the indie game project

The team needs to select a soundtrack style for an indie game with a fantasy setting. The soundtrack should support immersion, fit a moderate budget, and be feasible to produce within three months.

## Options

- A) Orchestral Fantasy Score — style: orchestral; mood: epic and immersive; production time weeks: 12; budget usd: 8000; instrumentation: live strings and brass (+ Creates a rich, cinematic atmosphere that enhances the fantasy setting; − Higher production costs and longer recording sessions may strain the budget)
- B) Chiptune Retro Soundtrack — style: chiptune; mood: nostalgic and playful; production time weeks: 6; budget usd: 2000; instrumentation: synthesized 8-bit sounds (+ Faster and more affordable production with a distinctive retro vibe; − May not fully match the immersive fantasy atmosphere desired)
- C) Ambient Electronic Soundscape — style: ambient electronic; mood: mysterious and atmospheric; production time weeks: 8; budget usd: 4000; instrumentation: synthesizers and sound design (+ Supports immersion with subtle, mood-enhancing textures; − Less melodic content might reduce emotional engagement for some players)
