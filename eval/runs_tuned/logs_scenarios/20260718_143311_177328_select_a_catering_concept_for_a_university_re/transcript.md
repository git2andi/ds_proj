# Select a catering concept for a university research symposium

A university research symposium requires a catering concept to serve approximately 150 attendees during a 4-hour event. The options must accommodate dietary restrictions and fit within a moderate budget.

## Options

- A) Buffet Style with Hot and Cold Dishes — serving style: self-serve buffet; dietary options: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free; meal type: hot and cold dishes; budget range: moderate; guest capacity: 150 (+ Allows attendees to choose from a variety of dishes at their own pace; − Requires space for buffet setup and may cause lines during peak times)
- B) Boxed Lunches with Mixed Sandwiches and Salads — serving style: individually boxed; dietary options: vegetarian, gluten-free; meal type: cold sandwiches and salads; budget range: moderate; guest capacity: 150 (+ Easy distribution and convenient for attendees to eat while networking; − Limited hot food options and less variety in meal types)
- C) Passed Hors d'oeuvres with Beverage Stations — serving style: waitstaff passed small plates; dietary options: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free; meal type: small bites and appetizers; budget range: moderate; guest capacity: 150 (+ Encourages mingling and offers a variety of bite-sized options; − May not satisfy attendees looking for a full meal)
- D) Food Truck Catering with Diverse International Cuisine — serving style: food trucks on-site; dietary options: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free; meal type: varied international dishes; budget range: moderate; guest capacity: 150 (+ Provides diverse meal options and a casual atmosphere; − Weather dependent and requires outdoor space for setup)
