# Select a theme for the annual science communication workshop

The annual science communication workshop requires selecting a theme that engages diverse audiences and enhances participants' skills. Four theme options are proposed, each focusing on different communication strategies and scientific topics relevant to current trends.

## Options

- A) Storytelling Techniques in Science Communication — focus area: narrative skills; target audience: general public; session count: 5; interactive elements: role-playing (+ Enhances participants' ability to connect emotionally with audiences; − May require more preparation time for role-playing activities)
- B) Visual Communication and Data Visualization — focus area: visual tools; target audience: scientists and educators; session count: 4; interactive elements: software tutorials (+ Improves skills in presenting complex data clearly; − Participants may need prior experience with visualization software)
- C) Communicating Science in Social Media — focus area: digital platforms; target audience: young adults; session count: 3; interactive elements: content creation challenges (+ Prepares participants to engage audiences on popular social channels; − Limited session count may restrict depth of coverage)
- D) Ethics and Accuracy in Science Communication — focus area: ethical standards; target audience: science communicators; session count: 4; interactive elements: case study discussions (+ Strengthens understanding of responsible communication practices; − Discussions may be less engaging for participants seeking practical skills)
