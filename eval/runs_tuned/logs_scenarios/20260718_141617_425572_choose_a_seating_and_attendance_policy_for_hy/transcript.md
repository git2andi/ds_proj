# Choose a seating and attendance policy for hybrid lectures

The group must decide on a seating and attendance policy for hybrid lectures involving 7 participants. The policy should accommodate both in-person and remote attendance while ensuring clear guidelines on seating arrangements and participation requirements.

## Options

- A) Assigned Seating with Mandatory In-Person Attendance — attendance mode: in-person required; seating arrangement: fixed assigned seats; remote participation: not allowed; attendance tracking: manual check-in (+ Ensures consistent seating and full in-person engagement; − Excludes remote participants and reduces flexibility)
- B) Flexible Seating with Optional Remote Attendance — attendance mode: in-person or remote; seating arrangement: open seating; remote participation: allowed anytime; attendance tracking: digital check-in (+ Offers maximum flexibility for all participants; − May cause unpredictable seating and variable in-person presence)
- C) Rotating Assigned Seating with Hybrid Attendance Requirement — attendance mode: hybrid required weekly; seating arrangement: rotating assigned seats; remote participation: allowed on rotation; attendance tracking: automated and manual (+ Balances in-person and remote participation fairly; − Requires coordination and may complicate scheduling)
- D) Open Seating with Mandatory Weekly In-Person Session — attendance mode: remote allowed except one weekly in-person; seating arrangement: open seating; remote participation: allowed except mandatory session; attendance tracking: weekly attendance logs (+ Supports remote flexibility while maintaining some in-person interaction; − May reduce continuity in seating and group cohesion)
