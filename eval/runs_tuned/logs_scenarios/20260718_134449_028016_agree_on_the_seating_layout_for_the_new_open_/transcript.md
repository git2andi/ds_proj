# Agree on the seating layout for the new open-plan office

The team of 7 is deciding on a seating layout for their new open-plan office. The layout must accommodate individual workspaces, collaboration areas, and easy access to shared resources within a 100 square meter space.

## Options

- A) Clustered Pods Layout — workspace type: pods; pod size: 3-4 seats; collaboration area: central; shared resources access: within 5 meters (+ Encourages small group collaboration while maintaining personal space; − Pods may limit visibility across the entire office)
- B) Linear Desk Rows Layout — workspace type: rows; desk count per row: 7; collaboration area: side corner; shared resources access: distributed evenly (+ Maximizes line of sight and simplifies movement through the office; − Less informal interaction due to linear seating arrangement)
