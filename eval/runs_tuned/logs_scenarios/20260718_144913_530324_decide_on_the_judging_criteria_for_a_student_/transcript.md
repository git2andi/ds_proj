# Decide on the judging criteria for a student robotics competition

A group of five participants must decide on the judging criteria for a student robotics competition. The criteria should fairly evaluate robot performance, design, innovation, and teamwork within the competition's scope.

## Options

- A) Performance-Focused Criteria — criteria count: 3; criteria: ['speed', 'accuracy', 'task completion']; weight distribution: 70% performance, 30% other; evaluation method: quantitative scoring (+ Emphasizes measurable robot capabilities and task success; − May undervalue creativity and teamwork aspects)
- B) Balanced Criteria with Design and Innovation — criteria count: 5; criteria: ['performance', 'design', 'innovation', 'teamwork', 'presentation']; weight distribution: equal weights across criteria; evaluation method: mixed quantitative and qualitative (+ Provides a comprehensive evaluation covering multiple important aspects; − More complex to score and requires diverse judging expertise)
- C) Creativity and Teamwork Emphasis — criteria count: 4; criteria: ['innovation', 'teamwork', 'design', 'problem solving']; weight distribution: 60% creativity and teamwork, 40% performance; evaluation method: qualitative scoring with judges' feedback (+ Encourages original ideas and collaboration among students; − Less focus on objective performance metrics may affect fairness)
- D) Simplified Performance and Reliability Criteria — criteria count: 2; criteria: ['task reliability', 'performance consistency']; weight distribution: 100% performance-based; evaluation method: quantitative timed trials (+ Easy to administer and clearly objective; − Ignores design, innovation, and teamwork elements)
