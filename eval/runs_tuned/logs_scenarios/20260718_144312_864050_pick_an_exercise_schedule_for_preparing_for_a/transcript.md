# Pick an exercise schedule for preparing for a hiking holiday

Three participants need to choose an exercise schedule to prepare for a 5-day hiking holiday in moderate mountain terrain. The schedule should build endurance, strength, and flexibility over 8 weeks with 3 sessions per week.

## Options

- A) Interval Hiking and Strength Training — duration per session minutes: 90; session type: outdoor hiking and gym strength; frequency per week: 3; focus areas: endurance, leg strength, core stability (+ Combines real hiking practice with muscle strengthening exercises; − Requires access to gym equipment and outdoor trails)
- B) Indoor Cardio and Flexibility Routine — duration per session minutes: 60; session type: treadmill walking, cycling, yoga; frequency per week: 3; focus areas: cardiovascular fitness, flexibility, joint mobility (+ Can be done regardless of weather and improves flexibility; − Less specific hiking endurance training and outdoor exposure)
- C) Long Distance Walking with Weighted Backpack — duration per session minutes: 120; session type: outdoor walking with backpack; frequency per week: 3; focus areas: endurance, load carrying, leg muscle conditioning (+ Simulates actual hiking conditions and load carrying; − Long sessions may be tiring and require suitable terrain)
- D) Mixed Cross-Training and Stretching — duration per session minutes: 75; session type: cycling, bodyweight exercises, stretching; frequency per week: 3; focus areas: overall fitness, strength, flexibility (+ Varied exercises reduce monotony and improve multiple fitness areas; − Less direct hiking practice and may not fully prepare for terrain)
