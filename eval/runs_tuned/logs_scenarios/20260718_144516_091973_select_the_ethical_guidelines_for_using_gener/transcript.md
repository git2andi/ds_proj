# Select the ethical guidelines for using generative AI in coursework

The group must select ethical guidelines for using generative AI in coursework to ensure academic integrity and fairness. The guidelines should address transparency, originality, collaboration, and appropriate AI tool usage.

## Options

- A) Mandatory AI Disclosure and Citation — transparency required: true; originality check: manual and software; collaboration policy: individual work with AI assistance; tool restriction: only approved AI platforms (+ Promotes honesty and clear attribution of AI contributions; − May increase workload for instructors verifying AI use)
- B) Prohibit Generative AI in Individual Assignments — transparency required: not applicable; originality check: focused on non-AI content; collaboration policy: no AI use allowed; tool restriction: generative AI banned (+ Simplifies evaluation by excluding AI-generated content; − Limits students’ opportunity to learn responsible AI use)
- C) Allow AI Use with Reflective Reporting — transparency required: true with reflective statements; originality check: mixed manual and software; collaboration policy: AI as a collaborative tool; tool restriction: open AI tool usage (+ Encourages critical thinking about AI’s role in work; − May complicate grading due to varied AI involvement)
- D) Integrate AI Literacy and Usage Training — transparency required: encouraged but not mandatory; originality check: standard plagiarism checks; collaboration policy: guided AI use with instructor support; tool restriction: recommended AI tools with training (+ Supports ethical AI use through education and guidance; − Requires additional resources for training and monitoring)
