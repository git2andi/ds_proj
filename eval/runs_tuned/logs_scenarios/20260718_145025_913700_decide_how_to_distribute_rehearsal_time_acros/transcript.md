# Decide how to distribute rehearsal time across a theater production

A theater production team of 7 must allocate 20 total hours of rehearsal time. The goal is to balance individual practice, group scenes, and technical run-throughs to prepare efficiently for opening night.

## Options

- A) Equal Time for All Scenes — total hours: 20; scene types covered: 3; hours per scene type: 6.66; focus: balanced rehearsal (+ Ensures all scene types receive equal attention; − May not address more complex scenes needing extra practice)
