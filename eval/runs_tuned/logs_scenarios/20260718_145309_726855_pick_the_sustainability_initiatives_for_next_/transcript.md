# Pick the sustainability initiatives for next year's campus plan

The campus sustainability committee will select initiatives for next year's plan focusing on environmental impact, cost, and community engagement. Seven participants will decide among options that align with the campus's goals to reduce carbon footprint and promote green practices.

## Options
