# Choose the release schedule for an independent music EP

A group of six independent musicians must decide on a release schedule for their collaborative EP. The schedule should accommodate promotional activities and maximize audience engagement over a three-month period.

## Options

- A) Simultaneous Full EP Release — release type: full EP; release duration days: 1; promotion period days: 90; audience engagement style: intensive initial (+ Generates immediate full content access and concentrated promotional efforts; − May limit prolonged audience engagement and reduce sustained media attention)
- B) Monthly Single Track Releases — release type: single tracks; release duration days: 90; tracks per month: 1; promotion period days: 90; audience engagement style: steady drip (+ Maintains consistent audience interest and allows focused promotion per track; − Longer wait for full EP experience and potential listener fatigue)
- C) Biweekly Double Track Releases — release type: double tracks; release duration days: 42; tracks per release: 2; promotion period days: 60; audience engagement style: moderate pacing (+ Balances steady content flow with shorter overall release period; − Requires frequent promotional efforts and may overwhelm some listeners)
- D) Two-Part EP Release — release type: half EP each; release duration days: 60; parts: 2; promotion period days: 75; audience engagement style: staged anticipation (+ Creates anticipation between releases and allows focused marketing phases; − May disrupt listening continuity and split audience attention)
