# Choose a fantasy football draft strategy for the new season

The group of three fantasy football players must select a draft strategy for the upcoming season. Each strategy varies in risk level, player focus, and draft round emphasis to suit different team-building approaches.

## Options

- A) Early Running Back Focus — risk level: medium; primary position: running back; draft round focus: rounds 1-3; flexibility: low (+ Secures top-tier running backs early to anchor the team; − May limit options for other key positions in later rounds)
- B) Balanced Positional Approach — risk level: low; primary position: mixed; draft round focus: rounds 1-7; flexibility: high (+ Maintains roster balance by targeting multiple positions evenly; − Could miss out on elite players at any single position)
- C) Late-Round Quarterback Strategy — risk level: medium; primary position: quarterback; draft round focus: rounds 8-12; flexibility: medium (+ Allows focus on skill positions early while acquiring quarterback value later; − Risk of missing out on top-tier quarterbacks if others draft early)
- D) Wide Receiver Heavy Draft — risk level: medium; primary position: wide receiver; draft round focus: rounds 2-5; flexibility: medium (+ Builds a strong receiving corps to capitalize on passing offenses; − May underinvest in running backs, affecting rushing yard potential)
