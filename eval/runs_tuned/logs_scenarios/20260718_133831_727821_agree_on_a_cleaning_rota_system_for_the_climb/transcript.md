# Agree on a cleaning rota system for the climbing club hut

The climbing club hut requires a cleaning rota system to ensure regular maintenance. Six members will share cleaning duties, with each option outlining a different scheduling and responsibility approach.

## Options

- A) Weekly Rotation by Member Number — rotation frequency: weekly; assignment method: fixed order by member number; task distribution: equal tasks each week; duration per shift: 2 hours (+ Ensures predictable and equal workload distribution among members; − May not accommodate members’ varying availability each week)
- B) Monthly Team Cleaning Groups — rotation frequency: monthly; assignment method: two teams of three members; task distribution: shared within teams; duration per shift: 4 hours (+ Allows teamwork and flexibility within groups for task completion; − Longer intervals between cleanings could reduce hut upkeep consistency)
- C) Task-Based Assignment with Volunteer Sign-Up — rotation frequency: task-based; assignment method: volunteer sign-up each period; task distribution: specific tasks assigned individually; duration per shift: varies by task (+ Members can choose tasks that fit their skills and schedules; − Risk of uneven task coverage if volunteers are insufficient)
- D) Biweekly Shared Cleaning Sessions — rotation frequency: biweekly; assignment method: all members clean together; task distribution: divided during session; duration per shift: 3 hours (+ Promotes group cohesion and shared responsibility; − Scheduling a time that suits all six members may be challenging)
