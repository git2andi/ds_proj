# Choose the cover design for the self-published novel

The group is selecting a cover design for a self-published novel. Each design varies in style, color scheme, and illustration type, aiming to attract the target audience of young adult fantasy readers.

## Options

- A) Mystical Forest Illustration — style: hand-drawn; color scheme: dark green and purple; illustration type: detailed landscape; target audience appeal: fantasy enthusiasts (+ Creates an immersive fantasy atmosphere with intricate details; − May appear too complex for quick recognition on small thumbnails)
- B) Minimalist Symbolic Design — style: minimalist; color scheme: black and white; illustration type: abstract symbol; target audience appeal: modern design lovers (+ Simple and clean, easy to recognize at a glance; − Might not convey the fantasy theme clearly to some readers)
- C) Character Portrait Cover — style: digital painting; color scheme: warm tones; illustration type: main character portrait; target audience appeal: readers interested in character-driven stories (+ Connects readers emotionally by highlighting the protagonist; − Could limit appeal if readers prefer broader fantasy elements)
- D) Abstract Magical Elements — style: mixed media; color scheme: bright blues and golds; illustration type: floating magical symbols; target audience appeal: fans of mystical and magical themes (+ Visually striking with vibrant colors and dynamic composition; − Abstract nature may confuse readers about the novel’s genre)
