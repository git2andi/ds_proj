# Choose a workshop topic for the office learning day

The office learning day aims to enhance employees' skills through interactive workshops. Three participants will decide on one workshop topic that fits a 2-hour session and appeals to diverse professional interests.

## Options

- A) Effective Communication Skills — duration minutes: 120; format: interactive; target audience: all employees; required materials: none (+ Improves team collaboration and reduces misunderstandings; − May cover familiar concepts for some participants)
- B) Introduction to Data Analysis with Excel — duration minutes: 120; format: hands-on; target audience: employees with basic Excel knowledge; required materials: laptops with Excel installed (+ Enhances practical data handling skills relevant to many roles; − Requires participants to bring laptops and have prior Excel familiarity)
- C) Time Management and Productivity Techniques — duration minutes: 120; format: lecture and exercises; target audience: all employees; required materials: workbook handouts (+ Helps employees organize tasks and improve work efficiency; − Some may find techniques less applicable to their work style)
- D) Basics of Cybersecurity Awareness — duration minutes: 120; format: presentation and discussion; target audience: all employees; required materials: projector and internet access (+ Raises awareness of security risks and safe online practices; − Technical content may be challenging for non-technical participants)
