# Decide how to divide responsibilities for a student software project

## Options

- A) Divide by Expertise — front end dev: Student 1; back end dev: Student 2; tester: Student 3; estimated hours per week: 10 (+ Each member focuses on their strongest skill area; − May reduce cross-functional learning opportunities)
- B) Rotate Responsibilities Weekly — rotation period: 1 week; tasks covered: front-end, back-end, testing; estimated hours per week: 12 (+ Everyone gains experience in all roles; − Frequent switching may reduce efficiency)

- D) Lead and Support Roles — project lead: Student 1; support roles: Student 2 and Student 3; estimated hours per week: 11 (+ Clear leadership can improve decision-making; − Support roles may feel less engaged)
