# Decide how to reduce energy use in the department building

The department building uses approximately 50,000 kWh monthly. The group must select one method to reduce energy consumption while maintaining comfort and productivity for all occupants.

## Options

- A) Install motion sensor lighting in common areas — estimated savings kWh per month: 2000; initial cost usd: 1500; installation time days: 3; maintenance frequency per year: 1 (+ Reduces lighting use automatically when areas are unoccupied; − Sensors may occasionally fail to detect presence, causing inconvenience)
- B) Upgrade HVAC system to energy-efficient model — estimated savings kWh per month: 5000; initial cost usd: 25000; installation time days: 14; expected lifespan years: 15 (+ Improves heating and cooling efficiency, lowering overall energy use; − High upfront cost and longer installation may disrupt operations temporarily)
- C) Implement a strict thermostat policy with set temperature ranges — estimated savings kWh per month: 1500; policy enforcement frequency per month: 2; required training hours per employee: 1; comfort impact level: moderate (+ Encourages consistent temperature settings to reduce HVAC energy waste; − May cause discomfort for some employees during extreme weather)
- D) Replace all incandescent bulbs with LED bulbs — estimated savings kWh per month: 1800; initial cost usd: 4000; replacement cycle years: 10; installation time days: 5 (+ LED bulbs consume less energy and have longer lifespans than incandescent bulbs; − Initial replacement requires coordination and temporary lighting adjustments)
