# Decide between remote and in-person attendance for a professional conference

Two professionals must decide how to attend an upcoming three-day conference with networking sessions and workshops. Both remote and in-person attendance options are available, each with different costs, travel requirements, and interaction levels.

## Options

- A) Attend Fully In-Person — attendance mode: in-person; conference duration days: 3; travel required: True; registration fee usd: 800; networking opportunities: high (+ Direct face-to-face networking and full access to all sessions; − Travel time and expenses increase overall cost and time commitment)
- B) Attend Fully Remote — attendance mode: remote; conference duration days: 3; travel required: False; registration fee usd: 500; networking opportunities: limited (+ No travel needed and lower registration cost; − Limited informal networking and potential for technical issues)
