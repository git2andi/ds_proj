# Choose the mural motif for the school playground wall

The school playground wall will be painted with a mural to enhance the environment and reflect community values. Seven participants will decide on one of four mural motifs, each suitable for the outdoor wall and appealing to diverse age groups.

## Options

- A) Nature and Wildlife Theme — colors: green, blue, brown; elements: trees, animals, sky; style: realistic; age appeal: all ages (+ Promotes environmental awareness and connects children with nature; − May require frequent touch-ups due to detailed elements exposed to weather)
- B) Abstract Geometric Patterns — colors: red, yellow, black, white; elements: shapes, lines, symmetry; style: modern abstract; age appeal: older children and teens (+ Creates a vibrant, dynamic look that encourages creativity and interpretation; − Might be less engaging for younger children who prefer recognizable images)
- C) Local History and Culture — colors: earth tones, muted blues; elements: historical figures, landmarks, cultural symbols; style: illustrative; age appeal: all ages (+ Educates students about the community’s heritage and fosters local pride; − Requires research and approval to accurately represent cultural elements)
- D) Playful Cartoon Characters — colors: bright primary colors; elements: animals, kids, fantasy creatures; style: cartoon; age appeal: younger children (+ Engages younger children with fun, friendly imagery that encourages play; − May not appeal as strongly to older students or adults)
